# Changelog

## 2026-10-17
- Place / museum guides rewritten as a two-step default:
  1. Markdown package first — full research, ranked sources with provenance, dual-layer digests for every Core and Important space or work, themes (including location/institution history), glossary, further reading, limitations. Delivered as a zip.
  2. Visitor EPUB — after the Markdown package is audited, a polished sequential EPUB that restructures *the same content* for on-site use. The EPUB does not abbreviate the dual-layer analysis; it transcribes the full Pre-Reading Context, Summary, Full Brief (zone-by-zone and Period-Specialist Analysis), and theme narratives.
- Ranked unmissable works and spaces: do not artificially cap digests. Present a clear priority order (short route and full “you may never return” route) so the visitor can decide depth.
- Location and institutional context required for museum guides (building history, founding, dynasty or patronage, major political or cultural settlements).
- EPUB design: high-class museum-catalogue aesthetic, elegant cover, explicit Table of Contents, ranked must-see checklist, visitor information and walkthrough help integrated throughout.
- Process refined from the Kunsthistorisches Museum guide and the Sisi Museum / Imperial Apartments guide.
- Updated `README.md` and the Place / Location section of `SKILL.md` to match.

## 2026-10-16
- Added `README.md` for the skill.

Both the Markdown zip and the EPUB are delivered. Process distilled from the Kunsthistorisches Museum guide. See `references/place-guides.md`.

Expand the ranked lists when major additional holdings (Rembrandt, Caravaggio, Rubens, etc.) are essential. Document the expanded selection in `01-sources.md` and `limitations.md`.
