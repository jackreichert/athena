# Scientific Paper Deep Dives (Athena Adaptive Application)

Load this file when the topic is a primary research paper (or a tightly related set of papers), a journal-club style analysis, or an explicit request for scientific-paper reading / critique / positioning guidance. Orthogonal to package shape (Survey / Standard / Deep).

This adaptive application is grounded in the Discovery-first package on how strong scientific training programs actually teach these skills (CREATE strategy, Carey et al. ten rules, Swales CARS, Users’ Guides critical-appraisal tradition, and related pedagogical literature).

## Core orientation
Athena scientific-paper packages treat the primary research article as the object of study. The goal is to help the reader:
- Reconstruct the research question and the gap the authors claim to fill
- Independently interpret figures and tables before accepting the authors’ narrative
- Evaluate methods, controls, and the strength of evidence
- Distinguish results from interpretation
- Assess limitations, alternative explanations, and positioning claims
- Generate productive next questions or experiments

Digests and themes should enable both careful individual reading and journal-club / seminar participation.

## Source treatment
- The focal primary paper(s) are Core sources.
- Supporting methodological or related papers, key prior work cited by the focal paper, and high-quality reviews that map the debate may be ranked Important or Supplementary.
- When the request is meta (how to read papers in general), the pedagogical and practical guides themselves become Core (as in the discovery package).

## Digest adaptations for primary research papers
Use the standard three-part structure (Pre-Reading Context / Summary / Full Brief) with the following domain-specific emphases:

**Pre-Reading Context**
- Field and problem the paper enters
- Key prior results or controversies a reader should have lightly in mind
- Practical notes (open access status, length, density of figures, any supplemental material that is essential)
- What kind of claim the paper is likely making (mechanistic, observational, methodological, clinical, theoretical, etc.)

**Summary (Standalone)**
- Research question / hypothesis in one or two sentences
- Core approach and key result(s)
- Authors’ main interpretive claim
- One sentence on claimed contribution or positioning

**Full Brief (Post-Reading)** — denser and more critical
- Precise statement of the gap or niche the authors establish
- Methods and design choices that matter (including controls, sample, statistical approach, key assumptions)
- Figure-by-figure or table-by-table reconstruction: what the data actually show, independent of the authors’ prose
- Results vs. interpretation: where the paper moves from data to claims
- Strengths, limitations, and plausible alternative explanations
- Positioning: how the paper uses prior literature (CARS-style moves) and how subsequent work has treated it (when known)
- Generative close: what experiment, analysis, or follow-up question does this paper most clearly open?

**Deep-mode additions**
- Methodological / formal approach section
- Reception and subsequent engagement (citations, replications, critiques, extensions)
- Explicit note on reproducibility or open-data/code status when relevant

For multi-paper series (CREATE-style), produce linked digests and a short overarching synthesis note.

## Theme emphases
Recurring high-value themes for scientific-paper packages:
- Research question and gap construction
- Evidence quality and controls
- Figure literacy and independent data interpretation
- Claim strength and alternative explanations
- Positioning and contribution claims
- Limitations, generalizability, and next experiments
- (When relevant) Reproducibility, statistical practices, or disciplinary norms

Seminar Guiding Questions (Deep) should progress from accurate reconstruction → critique of methods/claims → comparison across papers → design of a follow-up study or alternative analysis.

## Active learning & deliverables
- Journal-club style discussion questions are preferred.
- Optional short exercises: “annotate this figure,” “write the next experiment,” “rewrite the authors’ claim more cautiously,” “map the CARS moves in the introduction.”
- For Deep packages, include a brief note on how an instructor might use the package in a real journal club or methods course.

## Images
When figures are central to the argument, include high-quality reference images of key figures (with credit and link to the original paper or publisher) under `images/`. Caption them so the reader can study the data even if the full paper is later closed.

## Relationship to shared spine
All standard Athena rules remain in force (syllabi/provenance when applicable, ranked sources, citation density in themes, further-reading pathways, anti-reductive stance). This file supplies only the domain overlays for primary scientific literature.
